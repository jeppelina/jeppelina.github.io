Publications

Peer-reviewed articles

2025

Cohabitation and Mortality Across the Life Course: A Longitudinal Cohort Study with Swedish Register-Based Sibling Comparisons

Lindmarker, J., Kolk, M., & Drefahl, S.

European Journal of Population 41:2. 10.1007/s10680-024-09722-6

Sibling fixed-effects survival models on 5.6 million individuals in Swedish registers, 2012 to 2017, separating selection into partnership from the association between partnership and mortality.

Under review

2026

However Far Away? The Spatial Contingencies of Assortative Mating

Lindmarker, J., & Jarvis, B. F.

Submitted to Demography, June 2026. Preprint on SocArXiv: 10.31235/osf.io/4tq8z_v1

Conditional logit models on Swedish registers, 1990 to 2017, comparing observed unions against counterfactual partners drawn from the local pool of singles. Between 20 and 40 per cent of same-ancestry partnering is accounted for by residential proximity alone.

Manuscripts

In preparation

Working Together, Living Together? The Opportunity Structure of Educational Homogamy

Lindmarker, J., & Jarvis, B. F.

Conditional logit models with KHB mediation decomposition on 1.25 million first unions, isolating university co-enrolment, workplace co-membership, and residential proximity. Full manuscript, targeted at Demography.

In preparation

Three Decades of Ethnic Assortative Mating in Sweden: Decomposing Trends into Structural Change and Assortativity Dynamics

Lindmarker, J.

Conditional logit models with an Oaxaca-Blinder decomposition on register data, 1991 to 2022, separating shifts in the partner market from changes in residual assortativity across six ancestry groups and two immigrant generations.

In preparation

Beyond Majority and Minority: Fine-Grained Ethnic Boundary Structures in Swedish Assortative Mating

Lindmarker, J., & Nordlund, C.

Two-sided matching simulation with log-linear models and Walktrap community detection across 2,450 ancestry-group pairings in full-population register data, 1991 to 2022.

Teaching

2025–

Statistics and Data Science II (771A17), 7.5 ECTS Course director

MSc programme in Computational Social Science, Linköping University Norrköping

Designed the course and teach it alone: eight lectures, eight labs, and a sit-in examination, covering regression, causal inference and DAGs, logistic and multinomial models, and discrete choice analysis. Cohort of around 22 students.

  • Assessment designed as labs (3.0 ECTS), an AI log (1.0 ECTS), and a written exam (3.5 ECTS)
  • The AI log is my own addition: students document and reflect on how they used generative tools in each lab
  • Rebuilt for 2026 after a constructive-alignment audit, with a new exam blueprint and an in-class teaching game
  • All slides and labs are published openly
