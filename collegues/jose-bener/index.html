About José

José is a designer and a PhD student, and he treats those two things as one craft.

His research lives in information visualization, visual analytics, and interaction design. He works in the Visualization and Interaction Design group at Linköping University, in the Media and Information Technology division on Campus Norrköping, as one of Professor Miriah Meyer's PhD students.

What draws him in is the human-centred side of data: how people actually read, question, and make sense of what they see. He brings critical perspectives into visualization and explores interaction design for understanding social contexts, so that a chart or a tool is not just correct but honest about whose story it tells.

He calls the things he makes digital playgrounds. The phrase is not decoration. It is a belief that good design invites curiosity, gives people room to explore, and rewards a little bit of mischief.

Before the PhD

Product design

Product designer at Albert

Before academia, José worked as a product designer at the EdTech company Albert, where he led product design for one of their main products. He works as a UX and UI designer across product design, user research, prototyping, design sprints, and usability testing, with a steady focus on education technology and on fostering creativity in children.

MFA & AHA Lab

Child Culture Design, Gothenburg

He holds an MFA in Child Culture Design from the University of Gothenburg. His thesis project, AHA Lab, ran twenty co-design sessions with children aged nine to thirteen, exploring digital technologies and electronics. That practice of designing with children, not just for them, carries straight into his research today.
