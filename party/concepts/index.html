PARTY DOCTOR PARTY: five directions

Click one to open it. Scroll down slowly: the head falls step by step onto a doctoral robe, becomes a data point on the plot (drag the slider), and lands at the RSVP in a party hat. Tap the head to make it flip through other photos.

1 · Rings

Concentric circles in plum and pink, like Figment. Your head pops out of the top of the disc.

2 · Cut-outs

Blue and paper-white cut-outs, like Duos. Your head is pasted into the collage between the glasses.

3 · Numbers

The date is the artwork, like Fives. Your head peeks out between 19 and 12. The details read like the back of a game box.

4 · Night

A December night sky of folk ornaments, like Lacuna. Your head sits in the arch where the sun was.

5 · Faces

A grid of little guests, like Wilmot's tiles. You are the guest of honour and everyone looks at you.
