Under the hood

Not a workout randomiser.

Every session is the output of a real model of your training, not a shuffle of exercises. A few of the moving parts:

Recovery model

It tracks 43 muscles

Freshness and weekly volume per muscle, not a blunt "chest day". That heatmap on the home screen is the state it plans from.

Volume science

Between too little and too much

Each muscle is steered inside its productive weekly range, from the minimum that grows to the most you can recover from.

Injury routing

34 movement stressors

Pain becomes mechanical stressors, then every candidate lift is checked against them and excluded, substituted, or eased.

Mesocycles

Deloads before you stall

Rising effort or repeated stalls trigger a lighter week automatically, halving volume and capping the effort.

Selection

Every lift is scored

Goal fit, equipment, recency, and your own more/less feedback combine into the pick, so it rotates without drifting.

Progression

It reads your last sets

The next set is suggested from how the recent ones actually went, nudging weight or reps and flagging anything odd.
